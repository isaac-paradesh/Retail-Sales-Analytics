# Business Insights

## 1. Technology leads product-category sales

Technology generated approximately **$827K in sales**, representing about **36.6% of total revenue**.

Furniture generated approximately **$729K**, while Office Supplies generated approximately **$705K**.

### Interpretation

Technology is the strongest product category by sales. The business could investigate which Technology sub-categories are responsible for this performance and whether high-performing products justify additional sales or inventory focus.

---

## 2. The West is the strongest sales region

The West generated approximately **$710K**, representing about **31.4% of total sales**.

The South generated approximately **$389K**, representing about **17.2% of total sales**.

West sales were therefore approximately **82.5% higher than South sales**.

### Interpretation

There is a substantial difference in regional sales performance. Management could investigate differences in customer mix, product demand or market coverage between the West and South to identify opportunities to improve weaker-region performance.

---

## 3. Consumer customers are the largest segment

The Consumer segment generated roughly **$1.15M in sales**, accounting for approximately half of total revenue.

Corporate customers were the second-largest segment, while Home Office generated the lowest sales of the three segments.

### Interpretation

Consumer customers are particularly important to overall revenue. Retention, targeted promotions and product strategies aimed at this segment could therefore have a meaningful impact on business performance.

---

## 4. Sean Miller is the highest-value customer

Sean Miller generated approximately **$25.0K in sales**, making him the highest-value customer in the dataset.

Tamara Chand generated approximately **$19.1K**, meaning Sean Miller generated about **31% more sales** than the second-ranked customer.

### Interpretation

Customer-level analysis can identify the business's most valuable customers and reveal the categories and products they purchase. This could support targeted retention and relationship-management activity.

---

## Summary

The dashboard identifies three major drivers of sales performance:

- **Technology** leads category revenue.
- **West** leads regional sales.
- **Consumer** is the largest customer segment.

The Customer Details page then allows these high-level findings to be investigated at individual-customer and product level.
